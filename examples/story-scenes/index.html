Stonebridge Builders

Watch a six-storey block go up in the time it takes to read how.

  1. We start underground.

    Before a wall goes up, five rows of piles go down to rock. The slab is poured in one continuous day, so there is no cold joint for water to find.

  2. Then the skeleton.

    The steel frame is bolted, not welded on site. Scaffold rises with it, one lift at a time, so the crew never works more than a floor above a guardrail.

  3. A floor a week.

    Floors are cast in our yard and craned into place. Six weeks, six floors, and the site next door never hears a concrete mixer.

  4. Lights on before handover.

    Every circuit is switched on and every window tested at dusk, floor by floor, while we can still fix what we find.

  5. The crane leaves. You move in.

    Scaffold down, roof garden planted, keys handed over. Fourteen months from the first soil test.

Kettle Road Tea Co.

From a hillside in Nuwara Eliya to a kettle in Bristol, 9,000 km in four stops.

  1. Picked at 1,900 metres.

    Two leaves and a bud, by hand, before the sun is high. Cold nights up here slow the leaf down and give it its bright, brisk cup.

  2. Withered overnight.

    The leaf rests on long troughs of warm air in the estate's own shed, then is rolled, oxidised and fired the same morning.

  3. Shipped from Colombo.

    Sealed chests leave port within a week of picking. We buy direct, so the estate is paid before the ship sails.

  4. Blended in Bristol.

    We taste every lot against last season's before it goes in a tin. If it isn't better, it isn't sold.

Halden Audio

Six parts, no glue. Every one of them yours to replace.

  1. Halden One, opened up.

    Four screws hold the whole speaker together. Take them out and it comes apart in the order you see here.

  2. Sound first.

    A steel grille over a 90 mm driver: the only two parts that decide how it sounds, and the only two we'll never change between models.

  3. The parts that wear out.

    The battery and amp board slide out on rails. A new battery costs less than a dinner out and takes two minutes to fit.

  4. A body built to outlast them.

    Recycled aluminium on a cork base. We'll take it back when you're done and melt it into the next one.
